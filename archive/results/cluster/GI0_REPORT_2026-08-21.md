# GI-0 cluster result: structural invariance is viable

Date: 2026-08-21  
Status: passed exploratory engineering gate; not claim-eligible  
Training performed: no

## Result

The CMWM causal-event graph representation passed its declared GI-0 gate on
CLSP. Across 65 generated episodes, the verifier recovered exactly five
whole-episode structural classes, one for each generator template. Every class
was template-pure, and all five node-renaming tests preserved both the 1-WL
fingerprint and exact label-preserving isomorphism.

For three-action local motifs, the training set contained 13 WL buckets and 13
exact equivalence classes. Validation coverage was 100%. The two held-out test
families had mean per-episode exact-verified training-motif coverage of
55.1515%, ranging from 46.6667% to 63.6364%. All 140 test motifs returned by the
WL index were exact matches; this fixture produced zero WL-candidate false
positives.

This supports a later, separately preregistered comparison of ordinary
content-addressed retrieval with structural motif retrieval. It does not show
that graph retrieval improves memory, prediction, or question answering. The
audit used simulator causal labels as ground truth only; those labels were not
given to a model.

## Decision

Keep exact graph isomorphism as an audit and possible retrieval-verification
mechanism. Do not place it in Prototype 1. The next permitted step is a GI-1
protocol draft with equal bytes, top-k, input evidence, and latency. It cannot
run until the existing E1 gate sequence authorizes a separate structural
retrieval study.

Do not train a GIN yet. Under standard message passing, GIN is tied to the
discriminative power of 1-WL; a learned encoder is not a substitute for exact
verification. Natural or observation-inferred graphs will also be noisier than
these generator-label graphs, so success on GI-0 should not be extrapolated to
FilmFest, Sherlock, or learned causal edges.
